# Floatem v0.6.0 Release Notes

> Historical release snapshot. For current behavior and support status, see [1.1.0 release state](./v1.1.0.md).

Floatem v0.6.0 refreshes the visual system and makes note, todo, floating-card, and desktop-card interactions feel consistent across the app.

## Themes and appearance

- Adds the Afterglow theme with warm gradients, ambient glow, subtle stars, rainbow texture, and native code-rendered decoration.
- Keeps Afterglow on one consistent light palette and removes the previous Night theme and time-based theme switching.
- Reorders the theme selector to Classic, Afterglow, Plum, Orchid, Forest, and Chrysanthemum.
- Keeps the initial note and todo card surface on the classic light palette across themes.

## Notes

- Moves grouping into the group label and places the edited time and compact actions on the same header row.
- Attaches the formatting toolbar directly to the editor and shares its folded state between panel and floating views.
- Strengthens assigned-group styling with colored borders, rails, gradients, and texture.
- Replaces the heavy note deletion effect with a smooth red single-burst shatter animation.

## Floating and desktop cards

- Preserves rounded corners through native drag previews and floating-window creation.
- Adds persistent desktop cards with a diagonal pushpin control and restores their position and custom size after relaunch.
- Scales text, controls, spacing, and decoration with custom floating-card dimensions.
- Allows horizontal and diagonal resizing while rejecting vertical-only resizing for notes and todos.
- Shrinks folded floating notes to the title and top row, then restores the previous expanded size.
- Keeps dialogs usable beside floating cards and improves drag-to-dock reliability.

## Todos and guidance

- Lets users press Enter on the Todos page to focus quick entry, type a todo, and press Enter again to submit.
- Updates the bilingual user guide for the current theme, note, todo, floating-card, desktop-card, and resize behavior.
- Removes keyword chips from the first-level guide menu.

## Settings and maintenance

- Adds and documents launch-at-login controls on macOS.
- Removes obsolete automatic-theme persistence fields from macOS and Windows storage.
- Expands integration coverage for themes, note groups, floating-card editing, resizing, folding, desktop pinning, and Todo quick entry.

## Platform support

- Floating notes, floating todos, and persistent desktop cards remain macOS-only.
- Windows keeps the regular Notes and Todos experience without native floating cards.
